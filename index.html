    Hand Song

    Make music with your hands, using your webcam.

    • Left hand: chord signs. Every chord has its own hand shape: ☝️ is C, ✌️ is G, 🤘 is Am and so on. The full chart is on screen, at the bottom left.
    • Right hand: strum. Sweep your hand down or up across the strings on the right. Sweep faster to strum harder.
    • Fist: Close your left hand to mute the strings.
    • Songs: Pick a song in the toolbar, make each chord's sign and strum along while you sing.
    • Styles: Pick a style to set the sound, beat and tempo in one go.
    • To play chords and a melody with finger counts or pinches instead, switch Mode to Count fingers or Pinch & slide in the toolbar.

    Keyboard: Space beat on/off · R record
